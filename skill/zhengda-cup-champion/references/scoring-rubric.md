# Champion scoring rubric

## Purpose

Use this 100-point internal rubric for diagnosis and iteration. It is a stricter cross-stage benchmark, not a claim that every organizer uses identical weights. Replace weights only when a scoped official score sheet is available.

## Rubric

| Dimension | Weight | Full-credit evidence | Typical severe defect |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Decision problem and topic value | 10 | Real decision owner, timely evidence gap, feasible scope | Topic is fashionable but no decision changes |
| Desk and qualitative foundation | 8 | Authoritative sources and authentic exploratory evidence shape constructs | Literature collage or invented interviews |
| Sampling and instrument design | 18 | Defined population/frame, justified sample, mapped and piloted instrument | Convenience sample presented as representative |
| Fieldwork and data quality | 14 | Reproducible collection, provenance, quality controls, cleaning log | Raw data absent or exclusion rules improvised |
| Analysis and model validity | 18 | Methods fit questions/design; diagnostics and uncertainty reported | Model stacking, leakage, unsupported causality |
| Findings and insight | 10 | Findings are material, segmented on defensible axes, quantified, limitation-aware, and integrated into a small set of named non-obvious insights | Restates charts, invents decorative personas, or leaves every model as a separate conclusion |
| Recommendations and implementation | 10 | Recommendation structure mirrors the findings; each action names evidence, owner, priority, KPI, risk, pilot, and stop rule | Generic publicity or policy slogans, or actions detached from the segment evidence |
| Report and visual communication | 6 | The report carries the full evidence burden while the deck rebuilds a concise decision story; figures are traceable, accurately encoded, consistent, and legible at delivery size | Miniaturized report pages, inconsistent numbers, misleading encoding, decorative clutter, identity drift, or unreadable rendering |
| Presentation and defense | 4 | The deck spends its attention on differentiated findings and actions; each slide has one job, and the team can defend the mother insight, evidence, limitations, and trade-offs | Cannot explain sample, method, segment logic, visual encoding, action mapping, or design choices |
| Compliance and integrity | 2 | All hard rules pass; AI, asset provenance, rights, and anonymity are verified | Any disqualifying violation or unapproved identity leak |

## Score caps

Apply caps before interpreting the total:

- Fabricated data, interviews, citations, or results: fail.
- Disqualifying official-rule violation: fail or cap at non-advancing.
- No credible data provenance: cap at 59.
- Population inference from unjustified convenience sampling: cap at 69 until claims are corrected.
- Major result cannot be reproduced: cap at 69.
- A named model does not match the executed algorithm, bound dataset, features, or displayed output: cap at 69 until the implementation identity and results are rebuilt.
- Headline sample, numerator, denominator, category, or cluster counts cannot be reconciled: cap at 69 when the finding changes; otherwise cap at 79 until the fact registry is corrected everywhere.
- A simulated or assumed scenario is presented as observed, validated, or realized impact: cap at 69 until every claim is relabeled and the recommendation is separated from evidence.
- Recommendations are not evidence-linked: cap at 79.
- Report and PPT materially disagree: cap at 79.
- A headline visual materially misrepresents data, denominator, scale, uncertainty, or evidence status: cap at 79 until corrected.
- A confirmed school, team, teacher, EVI-X, or other prohibited identity signal appears in an anonymous submission: fail or cap at non-advancing according to the scoped official rule.

## Visual quality gate

Visual polish is eligible for credit only after evidence, inference, and compliance gates pass. Verify the rendered artifact, not only editable sources. Require:

- an explicit `visual.mode` and anonymity status;
- traceability from every substantive visual to data, calculation, or cited source;
- truthful axes, areas, color scales, denominators, uncertainty, and segment labels;
- legible text, contrast, hierarchy, spacing, image resolution, and font rendering at delivery size;
- consistent use of approved tokens and, for IP work, the approved character master;
- asset provenance, rights status, AI-use disclosure, and human review;
- no EVI-X branding in an anonymous competition submission.

## Internal readiness interpretation

- 90-100: internally release-ready after independent verification and all scoped hard gates.
- 84-89: strong internal readiness; repair remaining high-risk issues before handoff.
- 76-83: credible project with material differentiation or rigor gaps.
- 65-75: major internal design or evidence-chain weaknesses.
- Below 65: rebuild core stages before polishing.

These bands diagnose the current evidence package; they do not retrospectively re-judge an awarded work or predict first prize, second prize, advancement, or national-final placement. State uncertainty, field strength, and stage-specific differences.

## Review output

For each dimension provide:

1. score and confidence;
2. artifact evidence;
3. deduction reason;
4. minimum fix;
5. championship-level enhancement;
6. regression test.
