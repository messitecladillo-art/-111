## Direction 2 — simplified themed contour

> Original A4 portrait report perimeter concept for {topic}, using a simplified contour inspired by {one chosen physical or geographic motif}, without maps, borders, identifiable buildings, official marks or a literal collage of icons. Colour palette {primary}, {secondary}, {accent}. Keep the same body and protected rectangles: {geometry}. A few elegant theme accents occupy opposite corners; the narrow perimeter connects them with restrained interrupted lines. Transparent centre, no content across the protected zone. No type, numbers, statistics, brand or award symbols. Flat clean shapes with controlled density and clear edges. Maintain a stable reading focus, especially when a dense page is placed inside.

## Iteration

After viewing both concepts, change one main variable per iteration: corner size, continuous line rhythm, motif density, or colour strength. Preserve the chosen theme grammar. For body/chapter/cover, vary emphasis while retaining the same coordinate system and text area. A cover can use a stronger perimeter; it is not permission to occupy its protected title area.
